# Free APA DSM-5-TR Assessment Measures for Adults — Guide and Links

Keywords: assessment measures, which assessment measures should I give after the first session, first diagnostic interview, Level 1 Cross-Cutting, WHODAS 2.0

Source: American Psychiatric Association, DSM-5-TR Online Assessment Measures: https://www.psychiatry.org/psychiatrists/practice/dsm/educational-resources/assessment-measures

## How to use this guide
· After the first diagnostic interview, recommend measures based on what came up. Start with the Level 1 Cross-Cutting measure and WHODAS 2.0, then add the Level 2 or disorder-specific measures that fit.
· Always link to the forms on the APA's own website (links below). Do not reproduce, quote, retype, or summarize the items or scoring of any form. The counselor downloads and prints the form or gives the client the link.
· The APA permits clinicians to reproduce these measures for use with their own patients. Some forms are owned by other organizations (PROMIS, WHO, Elsevier, NIDA, Dr. Goodman); the same clinician-use permission applies.
· Scores support clinical judgment and are never the sole basis for a diagnosis. These are adult forms; for clients under 18, recommend referral.
· When the counselor enters a completed score in the case file, help interpret it in the context of the interview, and suggest repeating severity measures to track progress.

## Start here (recommended for every adult client after the first interview)
· DSM-5 Self-Rated Level 1 Cross-Cutting Symptom Measure—Adult
  When to use: A broad screen across 13 symptom areas. Any area rated at or above the form's threshold points to the matching Level 2 measure below.
  Link: https://www.psychiatry.org/getContentAsset/e0b4b299-95b3-407b-b8c2-caa871ca218d/dfc3d011-8f63-43f6-9ed8-4b444333a1d0/APA-DSM5TR-Level1MeasureAdult?language=en
· WHODAS 2.0 (World Health Organization Disability Schedule 2.0, 36-item version, self-administered)
  When to use: Measures day-to-day functioning and disability (supports the impairment criterion and treatment planning).
  Link: https://www.psychiatry.org/getContentAsset/5f4794de-8d66-4434-99f3-784a3fd1e567/dfc3d011-8f63-43f6-9ed8-4b444333a1d0/APA-DSM5TR-WHODAS2SelfAdministered?language=en
