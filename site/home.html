What Is Belief Counseling?

Most of us assume our feelings come from what happens to us. Yet two people can face the same loss, the same criticism, or the same disappointment and come away feeling very differently. The difference is often what each person believes about what happened — about themselves, about other people, and about God.

Many of those beliefs are simply not true. They were learned early in life or during painful experiences, and they have been repeated so often that they now feel like facts:

  • “Something is fundamentally wrong with me.”
  • “I’m a failure because I lost my job.”
  • “If God loved me, I wouldn’t be struggling.”

Belief Counseling helps you find these false beliefs, test them honestly, and replace them with the truth of God’s Word. When the beliefs underneath change, the feelings and patterns built on top of them can change too.

About Dr. Campbell

Dr. Brian Campbell

Christian Belief Counseling

Dr. Brian Campbell began his career teaching in a doctoral program in clinical psychology, where he specialized in psychological assessment. After ten years, and a promotion to Associate Professor, he left the university for full-time private practice, where he provided over 100,000 hours of Christian counseling to thousands of hurting people.

Over those years, he saw that much of the pain they experienced was caused by beliefs they had never questioned, and that real change came when those beliefs were brought into the light of God’s truth. Belief Counseling grew out of that work. He is the author of Pearls: Scriptures to Live By, Godly Counsel: Scriptures for Today’s World, and Christlike: Walking the Walk.

Now retired from practice as a Florida-licensed psychologist, Dr. Campbell teaches graduate courses online on the integration of Christianity and psychology. Through this site, he is passing on what he has learned to people who are hurting and to the counselors who help them.
