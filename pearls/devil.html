Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 204–213.)

Lucifer (Satan/devil) was a powerful angel banished from heaven for wanting to be equal to God; he tempted Eve (and then Adam) to eat the forbidden fruit, and through Adam's disobedience all humans inherited a sinful nature. God sent Jesus to defeat the devil's work; Christ Himself was tempted in the wilderness but did not sin, and taught us to pray for deliverance from temptation. Satan is a liar and schemer who exploits our weaknesses, but Christians are never alone in the struggle — the Holy Spirit within us is stronger than Satan, and God will never let us be tempted beyond what we can bear, always providing a way out. The final decision to sin is our own choice, not God's doing. When Christ returns, Satan will be judged and thrown into hell forever.

God’s Guidance
— how to resist temptation: stay alert and keep your guard up; put on the “full armor of God” (truth, righteousness, readiness to share the gospel, faith, salvation, and the word of God as your sword); and pray, remembering that God always provides a way out of temptation.
