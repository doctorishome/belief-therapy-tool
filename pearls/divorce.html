Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 104–107.)

The Lord God Almighty hates divorce. He allowed divorce at only one point in history — in the time of Moses. Prior to that, divorce was not permitted. When Jesus began His ministry, He proclaimed that divorce was no longer permitted, with only one exception: “sexual immorality.” If a man or woman got divorced for any other reason and remarried, Jesus said he or she would be committing adultery. The apostle Paul later clarified that if a believer is married to an unbeliever who wants to stay, the believer may not divorce — but if the unbeliever leaves, the believer is no longer bound. Thus only two exceptions permit divorce: marital unfaithfulness, and abandonment by an unbeliever.

God’s Guidance
If you are divorced, thinking of divorce, or considering marrying someone divorced, seek Godly counsel — these situations are often complex and require thoughtful prayer and biblical guidance. Try not to be argumentative, and if you have children, make every effort to provide for them and for your ex-spouse; the scriptures suggest that failing to provide for one's immediate family is “worse than an unbeliever.”
