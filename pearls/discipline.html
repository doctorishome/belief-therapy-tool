Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 140–145.)

Christian parenting is to be based on love — love your children deeply from the heart, as God loves you; even parental mistakes are covered by love, since love never fails. One of the most important ways to show love is teaching children the Bible, hiding scripture in their hearts to help them resist sin. Be positive and careful in speech — “Speaking rashly is like a piercing sword” — and don’t embitter or exasperate your children or show favoritism.

Discipline is another expression of love; scripture advocates appropriate corporal punishment as a training tool (with the note that norms and laws vary today, and any physical discipline should be gentle, age-appropriate, and never abusive). Discipline is a blessing, just as God’s discipline of us is for our good. The goal of parenting isn’t for children to “like” their parents but to grow up to respect and obey them; properly disciplined children tend to live more righteous lives and bring their parents peace and joy.
