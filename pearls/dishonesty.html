Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 223–228.)

God sent the truth in the form of His Son, Jesus Christ, who proclaimed “I am the way and the truth and the life.” If you are a believer, you were chosen from the beginning of time to be saved through the sanctifying work of the Holy Spirit and belief in the truth. God examines whether we are walking in the truth; by contrast, it is the devil's plan that we believe lies, since he is the father of lies. The Bible is the primary source of truth for mankind — every word is flawless, perfect, and eternal. As we study God's word, our thinking and actions become purer, and we are “born again” through it; the truth literally “sets us free.”

To receive this transformation we must first accept the central truth of the gospel — that Christ died for our sins and rose again — which brings the indwelling Holy Spirit, the “Spirit of truth,” who guides us further into truth. There are always consequences for rejecting truth and following lies; once we receive the truth, we must hold on to it and not be swayed by those who try to distort it.
