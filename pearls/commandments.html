Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 188–196.)

We sin when we break God's moral law or fail to do what His teachings require. Lucifer (Satan) was the original source of sin, banished from heaven for wanting to be equal to God; he later tempted Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden, and sin entered the world through Adam's disobedience — every human since has inherited a sinful nature. No one is without sin; if we claim otherwise we deceive ourselves. Sin has consequences in this life even for forgiven Christians, though Christ's sacrifice spares believers the ultimate penalty of eternal separation from God — a man reaps what he sows.

God’s Guidance
without Christ, sin would hopelessly separate us from God; Christ was sent to pay the penalty for our sins, and the only requirement to have sin removed is to believe in Him and confess and repent. When we do, the Holy Spirit comes to dwell within us and gives us power to resist sin — we are no longer controlled by our sinful nature. Christians still struggle and sometimes fail, but the key is not giving up; deliberately continuing to sin while knowing the truth is dangerous, but through Christ's redeeming sacrifice, believers are ultimately set free from sin and dwell with the Lord for eternity.
