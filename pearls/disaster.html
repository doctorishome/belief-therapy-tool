Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 219–222.)

God does not promise a trouble-free world — we should expect problems and hardships. Sometimes our troubles come from our own sin, but not always; even righteous people experience hardship, and all of us have days of light and days of darkness. Whether facing poverty or natural disaster, God has not forgotten us — He is a refuge for the weak and needy, and Christians are called to defend the rights of the poor. Troubles aren't always “bad” — they can test our faith or serve as loving discipline; God urges us to be joyful and thankful in all circumstances regardless of what happens.

God’s Guidance
the most important thing to do in trouble is pray — pour out your heart to God and He will provide refuge, strength, and comfort. Don't be afraid; your problems are never too big for the Creator of the universe. Instead of focusing only on your own troubles, reach out to others and point them to the comfort God offers.
