Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 108–111.)

We are all sinners; no one is perfect except God alone. It was the mission of Christ to come to this earth to die for our sins. When Christ died for us on the cross, He took away all of our sins. Everyone who believes in Jesus Christ, and acknowledges that He died for our sins, receives complete forgiveness. From God’s perspective, forgiveness is “all-or-none” — God forgives each and every one of our sins, no matter how many or how big. Scriptures illustrate how complete God’s forgiveness is: He hides our sins behind His back, doesn’t remember them anymore, and removes them “as far as the east is from the west.”

God’s Guidance
Jesus made it very clear that in order to receive forgiveness from God, we must first forgive others — there is no “wiggle room.” As human beings, we often want to “pick and choose” whose sins we'll forgive or limit how many times, but scriptures indicate God does not allow this kind of selective forgiveness once someone repents and asks for forgiveness.
