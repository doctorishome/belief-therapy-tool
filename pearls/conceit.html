Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 167–170.)

God detests pride and promises the proud will one day be humbled; He sees our inmost thoughts, and His judgment can be harsh. Pride distances us from God and often leads to a person's downfall. Prideful people crave the praise of men more than the praise of God, and their self-love blinds them to their own sin; their haughtiness leads to arguments and an unwillingness to accept advice.

God’s Guidance
the antidote for pride is humility, a cardinal feature of Christ's character — though equal to God, He humbled Himself to become a servant and die for our sins. We are commanded to “clothe ourselves in humility” toward one another, never thinking ourselves better than others, and to honor others above ourselves. God favors the humble and the contrite in spirit; it is humility and fear of the Lord that bring wealth, honor, and life, and the meek who inherit the land and enjoy great peace.
