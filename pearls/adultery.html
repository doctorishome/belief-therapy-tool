Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 181–187.)

God’s plan is for men and women to marry, unite sexually, and become one flesh; the marriage bed is to be kept pure, with sex outside marriage prohibited. Adultery (the Seventh Commandment) is the most prominent sexual sin, and scripture also specifically condemns sex with prostitutes, homosexuality (called “detestable”), cross-dressing, bestiality, incest, and lustful looking (which Jesus equated with adultery of the heart).

God’s Guidance
Our bodies are a temple of the Holy Spirit, bought at the price of Christ’s sacrifice, and we are to honor God with them — fleeing sexual immorality, being self-controlled, and pursuing purity. Scripture reminds us we cannot hide sexual sin from God, since even our thoughts are known to Him. If caught in sexual sin, repent and turn back to the Lord; sincere repentance brings forgiveness and cleansing.
