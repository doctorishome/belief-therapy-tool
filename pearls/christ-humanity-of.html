Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 62–65.)

Some people have never heard of Jesus Christ. Others believe that he was a prophet, but not God—and that He never claimed to be God. Let’s listen to Jesus in His own words as He tells us who He is. These are either the outrageous claims of someone who is mentally ill, or these are the claims of the God of the universe. You must decide.
