Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 214–218.)

God is deeply concerned about our thinking — we cannot hide our thoughts from Him; He detests the thoughts of the wicked but is pleased with the pure in heart. As Christians we're born with a sinful nature but given free will to focus our minds on God and truth rather than evil; the sinful mind is hostile to God, while the Spirit-controlled mind leads to life and peace.

God’s Guidance
monitor your thoughts and reject those that run contrary to God's truth, taking every thought captive to make it obedient to Christ. Stop dwelling on the past and on things you can't change; focus on whatever is true, noble, right, pure, lovely, admirable, and praiseworthy, rather than conforming to the world's patterns of thought. Fix your thoughts on Jesus. When our thinking aligns with spiritual truth, healthy emotions naturally follow — the fruits of the Spirit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. Our beliefs shape our behavior; faith without action is dead, so we must live out our faith, especially through love for others.
