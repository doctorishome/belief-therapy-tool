Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 70–82.)

Our words, whether good or bad, flow out of the condition of our heart. Choose your words carefully, because on the day of judgment each of us will have to give an account for every careless word we have spoken. How to Speak: Be gentle and don’t shout. Raising your voice and being harsh with another person tends to “stir up anger” and break down communication. Control your anger; a hot-tempered man stirs up arguments and quarrels. When to Speak: Think before you speak. Sometimes it is much better to “hold your tongue” and listen to the other person before you answer. Avoid arguments and quarrels — if you love to quarrel, you probably love to sin.

What Not to Say: Don’t Stoop To The Other Person’s Level, Don’t Mock Others, Don’t Insult Others, Don’t Swear or Use Filthy Language, Don’t Lie — God detests people who tell lies, but He delights in people who are truthful. Don’t Tell Falsehoods — the Ninth Commandment states: “You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor.” Don’t Slander — God specifically commands us not to slander others. Don’t Gossip — gossip can prove to be extremely destructive with regard to human relationships; it can break up friendships, and “fuel the fire” of arguments and quarrels. Don’t Keep Bringing Up The Past — if the person you are talking to has sinned against you, but has genuinely repented, you should forgive him/her and stop bringing up the past offense; love keeps no record of wrongs.

God’s Guidance
What to Say: Be truthful — a person who tells lies may benefit temporarily, but “truthful lips” endure forever. Say Nice Things — kind and pleasant words can be very powerful, and can help heal any situation; they can be “sweet to the soul and healing to the bones.” Be Happy, Cheerful, Joyful — you can set the emotional tone for good communication. Encourage One Another — encourage each other daily, and build each other up; spur one another toward love and good deeds. Finally, strive to live a righteous and holy life, and let everything that comes out of your mouth reflect the faith you have in Jesus Christ. Be peaceable and considerate, and show true humility toward all men; in the end, those who walk uprightly will find peace.
