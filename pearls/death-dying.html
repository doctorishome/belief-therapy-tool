Synopsis

(Godly Counsel: pp. 83–90.)

If you are a Christian, you should not fear death. The day that you first believed in Jesus Christ is the day that you crossed over from death to life. All human beings have a physical body and a soul. The physical body of every human being is going to die at some point in time; no one has the power to control the day of his physical death. Death first entered into the world with the disobedience of Adam in the Garden of Eden; but just as Adam brought death to the world, Jesus Christ brought life. The resurrection of Jesus from the dead gives hope for all who believe in him.

When we die, our physical body will decay and go back to dust. Our spirit (or soul) will go immediately to heaven. For unbelievers, their souls will go immediately to hell, where they will await the final judgment day. At some future point in time, Jesus will return to this earth (the second coming) for a final judgment; spirit and flesh will be reunited and we will receive our glorified bodies, which will last forever in heaven. If you are a believer in Christ, death should not be fearful; only our physical body dies, not our spirit. Jesus Christ has defeated death and gone ahead of us to prepare a place for us in heaven.

If you are having trouble coping with death, try focusing your thoughts firmly on heaven, where we will be with Jesus and see Him face to face.

God’s Guidance
It is difficult to imagine how beautiful and wonderful heaven will be. We know that we will have bodies in heaven (our “glorified” bodies). Unlike our life on earth, there will be no more death or mourning, or crying or pain; there will be no sin in heaven and nothing there will be impure. There will be no darkness or night in heaven, and no sun or moon — the glory of God will give heaven its light.
