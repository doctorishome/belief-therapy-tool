Live Interactive Diagnosis Practice

AI-assisted practice cases

You interview a fictional client, played by an AI, and practice the work as you would in the room. You walk through the DSM-5-TR diagnostic process, and you practice Belief Counseling: identify a false belief, challenge it, and replace it with Scripture. An AI supervisor coaches you along the way, with questions to ask, feedback, and help with the diagnosis.

There are two sessions. Session 1 is the first interview: a functional analysis, a safety check, a diagnostic hypothesis, belief mining, the first false beliefs, and the assessment tools. Session 2 finishes the diagnosis, challenges and replaces the main false belief, and chooses the belief cards that fit. At the end, the AI gives you the diagnosis, feedback, and a report you can print.

Every client is fictional. This is training, not clinical care.

How to start a case

  1. Open Walt’s case page and click Print, so you have the case in front of you. Have a pen and paper ready for notes.
  2. Click ‘Save the AI case file.’ It saves to your computer’s Downloads folder (on a phone, to your Files).
  3. Open Grok, ChatGPT, or Claude and sign in to a free account.
  4. Start a new chat. Click the paperclip or ‘+’ beside the message box and choose Walt-AI-Case-File.txt from Downloads.
  5. Type OK and press Enter. The Supervisor welcomes you and the interview begins. Each reply starts with “Walt says:” or “Supervisor here:”. Type COACH anytime for help. If your messages run out, copy the “PICK UP HERE FOR SESSION 2” block into a new chat. You can type or use voice.
