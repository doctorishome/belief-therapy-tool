“My body has betrayed me.”

Where it comes from: A diagnosis, a flare, or a body that no longer cooperates can feel like treachery from the one thing we thought we could count on. Anger and grief at the body are natural, and over time they can turn into resentment, fear, or a sense of being at war with ourselves.
