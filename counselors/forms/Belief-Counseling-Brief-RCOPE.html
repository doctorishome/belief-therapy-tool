Scoring (counselor)

Each item is scored from 0 = not at all to 3 = a great deal. Positive religious coping is the sum of items 1–7 (0–21). Negative religious coping is the sum of items 8–14 (0–21). The article reports and interprets these two scores separately. This form does not add them together.

For counselors. Keep the two scores separate; do not add them together. A higher negative score (for example, feeling punished or abandoned by God) often points to false beliefs about God worth exploring in the Challenge and Replace steps.

The article scores extent of use from 0 (“not at all”) to 3 (“a great deal”) and uses “somewhat” for an interior response. “Quite a bit,” scored 2, is the customary label for that point; the 2011 article does not print those two words. Some published studies recode the same items from 1 to 4. This form keeps the 0–3 scoring.
