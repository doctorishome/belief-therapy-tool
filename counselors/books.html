Belief Counseling for Counselors

Books (Recommended)

Books Dr. Campbell recommends to counselors and their clients.

The Problem of Pain

C.S. Lewis

A classic Christian answer to why a good and all-powerful God allows pain.

When God Doesn't Make Sense

Dr. James Dobson

Help for believers whose suffering doesn't fit what they expected of God.

Pearls: Scriptures to Live By

Dr. Brian Campbell

A mental-health concordance of the Bible: Scriptures organized into 172 topics for the struggles people bring to counseling.

Godly Counsel: Scriptures for Today's World

Dr. Brian Campbell

Scripture lessons on 33 of life's most common struggles, such as anxiety, depression, anger, and grief.

Christlike: Walking the Walk

Dr. Brian Campbell, with Rev. Jim Angelakos

A picture of the mentally healthy Christian, based on the characteristics of Christ.

Cognitive Therapy of Personality Disorders

Aaron T. Beck, Denise D. Davis, and Arthur Freeman

A clinical reference on the core beliefs behind personality patterns.
