Frequently asked questions

How does sign-up and the free week work?
  1. Create your login with your email and a password.
  2. Enter a credit card at checkout.
  3. Nothing is charged during your 7-day free trial. Your membership starts at $29.95 a month on day 8 unless you cancel during the week. We’ll email you a reminder 3 days before your free week ends.

Cancel anytime in My Account → My Subscriptions. If you cancel after you’ve been billed, you keep access until the end of that billing month, and you won’t be billed again. The rest of that month isn’t refunded.

How do I cancel?

Sign in, then open My Account → My Subscriptions → Cancel. You can cancel anytime.

Will I get a refund if I cancel?

If you cancel during your free week, you pay nothing. If you cancel after you’ve been billed, you keep access until the end of that billing month, and you won’t be billed again. The rest of that month isn’t refunded. If you have special circumstances, contact us.

What do I need to run it?

A computer with an up-to-date web browser and an internet connection.

Can I enter real client information?

Use fictional or de-identified information unless your plan states otherwise.

Troubleshooting

I can’t sign in, or I forgot my password

On the Wix sign-in page, choose Forgot password.

I signed up, but I can’t get into the Counselors’ Room

Make sure you’re signed in with the same email you used at checkout. If you still can’t get in, email us at brian@beliefcounselor.com.

I was charged by mistake, or I was charged twice

Email us at brian@beliefcounselor.com with the date and the amount. We’ll look into it.

Clinical Companion is for practice

Clinical Companion’s AI is for practice, on fictional cases only. Try a practice case before using it with clients. The cases are fictional composites. Use fictional or de-identified information unless your plan states otherwise. Never enter real client names or details. Clinical Companion supports, and does not replace, your professional clinical judgment.
