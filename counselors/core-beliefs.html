Core False Beliefs

The deep-seated lies beneath much of our pain: lies about ourselves, other people, and the world.

Digging deeper

Many false beliefs are deep-seated and are difficult to unearth. For deeper toxic beliefs, please see: Core Beliefs, and Parental Messages.

Core false beliefs do not belong to any one level of Belief Mining. They often sit underneath the beliefs a client first describes, so they are worth looking for in every case.

The 22 core false beliefs

Grouped by theme. Each belief opens its own belief card, where the belief is challenged and replaced with truth from Scripture.

My Worth

  • Something is wrong with me.
  • I am worthless.
  • I am not lovable.
  • I am a failure.

My Strength

  • I am powerless.
  • I cannot handle this.
  • I cannot make it on my own.

Other People

  • I don’t belong.
  • People will leave me.
  • No one will care for me when I need them.
  • Nobody loves me.
  • People cannot be trusted.
  • My needs don’t matter.

The World & the Future

  • Things will always go badly.
  • The world is unsafe.
  • Something terrible is going to happen.

Rules I Live By

  • I must be perfect.
  • I must have everyone’s approval.
  • I must be in control.
  • I must not make mistakes.

After Painful Experiences

  • It was my fault.
  • What happened makes me dirty or bad.
