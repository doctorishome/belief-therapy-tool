Reason for Referral

Walter “Walt” Hendricks, 58, was referred by his family physician after a routine physical in October. The doctor found nothing medically wrong but wrote, “Patient seems flat and has lost weight. Says he is ‘just tired.’ Recommend counseling.” Walt asked for a Christian counselor. On the phone he said, “I’m only coming because my daughter made me promise.”

Background

White male, married 33 years, separated. Wife Linda moved out in mid-June and lives near their daughter Katie (29) in Chattanooga. Walt lives alone in the family home in a small north Georgia town. Band director at the same public high school for 31 years (“Mr. H”); his marching band won state twice. Plays trumpet. Directed his church’s adult choir until September. Older of two brothers. Father (deceased) was a Marine, later a plant foreman, “a hard man to impress.” Mother, 84, in assisted living nearby; Walt visits Sundays. One blood pressure pill daily for six years, unchanged. Normal physical and bloodwork in October, including thyroid. Nonsmoker. No previous counseling. Church member since boyhood; became a Christian at 12; calls his faith “the center of everything, until lately.” At intake: early, pressed shirt and school jacket, polite, jokes about being “sent to the principal’s office,” looks tired and thinner than his clothes.
