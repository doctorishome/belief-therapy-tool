Quick start

  1. From the Counselors’ Room, open Clinical Companion. It runs in your browser.
  2. Sign in with your member login.
  3. Try a practice case before you use it with clients.
  4. Write your session notes, then ask for an analysis.

Counselor information

Clinical Companion supports, and does not replace, your professional clinical judgment. Use fictional or de-identified information, and never enter real client names or details. It is meant for your own professional work.

Client Forms for Your Practice

Enter your practice details once. A New Client Packet and a Confidentiality Agreement open already filled in with those details. Review them with an attorney in your state.
