Important Disclaimer – Notice to Counselor

Confidentiality, duty-to-warn, mandated reporting, minors’ consent, and record-retention rules vary by state statute. These forms are general templates, not legal advice, and must be reviewed by an attorney familiar with the laws of your state and of any state where a telehealth client is located.

Notice to Minister

State laws vary. In many states, clergy are mandated reporters. Clergy privilege has limits and does not cover every conversation. Pastoral care is not licensed treatment: do not diagnose, and do not present yourself as a licensed counselor. Refer serious needs to a licensed professional. Have this agreement reviewed by an attorney in your state, and check it against your denomination’s policy.
