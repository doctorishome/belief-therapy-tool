About Dr. Campbell

Brian Campbell, Ph.D., has spent 35 years in private practice, teaching, and writing. Belief Therapy grew from that work: a Christian cognitive-behavioral approach that helps clients name the lies behind their pain and replace them with truth from God’s Word.
