How practice works

1
You choose a case.

Pick the type of case that matches your caseload. No random assignments.

2
You work each step.

The supervisor pauses and asks for your thinking before showing the model approach, and offers a help menu if you are stuck.

3
You keep a clean summary.

Each case ends with a summary of the model path and a short quiz you can review later.
