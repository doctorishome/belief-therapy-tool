Confidentiality with Children and Teens: A Guide for Counselors

Brian Campbell, Ph.D.

A practical guide to confidentiality when a child or teen is involved: mandated reporting of sexual, emotional, and physical abuse and neglect; corporal punishment versus abuse; prenatal substance exposure; age of consent and close-in-age laws; minors’ sexting; parental consent and custody; and how to tell a minor the limits of confidentiality, with worked question-and-answer examples. Laws differ from state to state, and this is general information, not legal advice.
