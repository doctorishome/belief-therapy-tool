Clinical background for the belief cards

Each public false-belief card links to a counselor note on origins, cognitive errors, and treatment approach. Members will find them organized by category: Spiritual/Religious, Psychological/Cognitive, Interpersonal, Socioeconomic, Sociocultural, and Biological/Physical.
