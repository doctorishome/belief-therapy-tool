Introduction

How the Counselors’ Room works, and where to begin.

Where to begin

  1. Open the Clinical Companion and complete the Foundations. Foundations 1 covers the basics (what a belief is, Belief Counseling, and Belief Mining). Foundations 2 is the counselor reading: history, ethics, and theory. Foundations 3 teaches the assessment skills: the DSM-5-TR diagnostic process and its practice tutorial.
  2. Read The Process and the Theory. These explain the three steps of Belief Counseling (Identify, Challenge, Replace) and why they work.
  3. Practice on a case study. Your first case is included. An AI supervisor trained on Dr. Campbell’s method guides you, one question at a time.
  4. Use the Clinical Companion with practice cases. Follow the 14 steps from the first phone call to the belief cards. Use fictional cases only until the secure version for real clients is ready.
