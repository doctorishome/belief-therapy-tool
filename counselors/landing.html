Who this site is for

This site is for licensed counselors, licensed psychologists, students training for professional degrees, and ministers who provide Christian counseling. It is written especially for professionals in private practice and for those who plan to open a practice.

Why Belief Counseling

While teaching and supervising graduate students, Dr. Brian Campbell saw the same problem again and again. Students were trained in many treatment models and began their careers unsure which one to use. Many of his colleagues felt the same way.

Belief Counseling is his answer. It builds on cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), the most extensively researched form of psychotherapy, with strong evidence across many of the disorders in the DSM-5-TR. In recent years researchers have begun integrating the Christian faith with CBT, an approach known as Christian cognitive-behavioral therapy (CCBT).

CCBT is still a young field, and many students and professionals are unsure how to apply it. Belief Counseling makes it clearer by keeping attention on the central concept in CCBT: beliefs. Psychological struggles are understood primarily as the result of irrational beliefs, or lies. The counselor helps the client identify those beliefs, challenge them, and replace them with the truth.

Two worldviews, one common ground

Most Christian counselors work between two worldviews. The secular worldview classifies and treats psychological disorders without reference to faith. The Christian worldview sees them through a spiritual lens. Dr. Campbell contends that the two share common ground in the concept of beliefs, which is central to the Christian faith and to modern CBT alike.

Christian counselors must also follow the laws of their state and the ethical rules of their licensing boards. In practice, that means knowing how to diagnose with the DSM-5-TR (the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders, Fifth Edition, Text Revision). Dr. Campbell has taught the DSM system in master’s and doctoral courses, and this site includes detailed training in it. You will also find the history of Christian CBT and where it stands in current research.

How to find your way around

A menu runs across the top of every page in the Counselors’ Room. It has six sections, in the order most counselors will want to read them:

  1. Belief Counseling. The basics: what a belief is, what Belief Counseling is, and who should practice it.
  2. The Process. The three steps (Identify, Challenge, and Replace), each with a short video, and the Belief Mining tools.
  3. Traditional Assessment. DSM-5-TR training, the telephone intake, the forms your client completes online, and the first interview. Start with the slide show called “The Flow.”
  4. Professional Tips. Tips, case studies, and theory.
  5. Library. Books, videos, and articles.
  6. Clinical Companion. The gold button. This is the working tool for your own cases, and it opens when you start your free week.

The Welcome page shows all six as boxes you can select. “Public Site” at the end of the menu takes you back to the main website. To come back to this Introduction later, choose “Introduction” on the Welcome page.

About the Clinical Companion

Diagnosis is complex, and helping clients identify and change faulty beliefs will be new to many counselors. The Clinical Companion uses artificial intelligence to help with both. You dictate or type your case notes, and it suggests follow-up questions that clarify the diagnosis and point to the beliefs that need to change.

If you have never started a private practice, or you want ideas for the one you have, the Clinical Companion walks you through the whole process one step at a time.

Your professional judgment comes first

The Clinical Companion offers suggestions only, with no guarantee that they are accurate. You remain fully responsible for each client’s diagnosis and for identifying the beliefs to be treated.

Practice cases only, for now

Please use fictional cases, or notes with every identifying detail removed. A secure version for real client records is being prepared.
