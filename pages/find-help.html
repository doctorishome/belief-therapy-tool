Find Help for What You're Going Through

Tell us in a few sentences what is troubling you. We will point you to the belief cards that may help, each with what Scripture says and simple steps you can take.

For example: "My husband left last year. I keep thinking it was my fault and that no one will ever want me again."

What you write is not saved or shared. It is only used to find cards for you.

These cards are for education and self-help and are not a substitute for professional counseling. If you are in danger or thinking about ending your life, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.
