Spiritual/Religious False Belief #64 · Heaven

“In heaven I’ll be grieving for the people who aren’t there.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Loving family members or friends who do not follow Christ. Fear that heaven cannot be happy if they are missing. Grief over a loved one who died without apparent faith. A tender heart that cannot imagine joy without the people you love. Not knowing how Scripture speaks about sorrow in heaven.

What Scripture teaches

This is a deep and loving concern, and Scripture does not answer every question about it. But it does promise that God will wipe away every tear and there will be no more mourning, crying, or pain. We will know fully, as we are fully known, and we will see God’s perfect justice and love clearly. The Judge of all the earth will do what is right. We can trust that heaven’s joy will not be shadowed by sorrow, even though we cannot yet understand how.

‘He will wipe away every tear from their eyes,’ and there will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the former things have passed away (Revelation 21:4).

Also see

He will swallow up death forever. The Lord GOD will wipe away the tears from every face and remove the disgrace of His people from the whole earth. For the LORD has spoken (Isaiah 25:8).

Far be it from You to do such a thing—to kill the righteous with the wicked, so that the righteous and the wicked are treated alike. Far be it from You! Will not the Judge of all the earth do what is right? (Genesis 18:25).

Now we see but a dim reflection as in a mirror; then we shall see face to face. Now I know in part; then I shall know fully, even as I am fully known (1 Corinthians 13:12).

O, the depth of the riches of the wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are His judgments, and untraceable His ways! (Romans 11:33).

Common self-statements

  • I can’t be happy in heaven without them.
  • Heaven will feel empty if they’re not there.
  • I’ll spend eternity missing someone.
  • How can God expect me to rejoice?
  • Heaven can’t be perfect if people I love are lost.
  • I’d rather not think about heaven at all.

Common logical errors

  • Fortune telling: Predicting what you will feel in heaven based on earthly limits.
  • Emotional reasoning: Concluding that because you feel this sorrow now, you must feel it forever.
  • Limited perspective: Judging eternity from partial knowledge.
  • Avoidance: Not thinking about heaven because the question hurts.

Challenging questions

  • What does Revelation 21:4 promise about tears and mourning?
  • Will I understand God’s ways more fully in heaven?
  • Can I trust the Judge of all the earth to do what is right?
  • Is it possible God can heal sorrow in ways I can’t imagine?
  • What can I do now for the people I love who don’t know Christ?
  • Can I grieve now and still trust God for later?
  • What do I know of God’s character that helps me trust Him here?

Truth summary

I cannot answer every question about heaven, but I can trust God’s promise: He will wipe away every tear, and there will be no more mourning. I will know fully, and I will see His justice and love clearly. The Judge of all the earth will do what is right. I can pray for the people I love today and trust God with what I don’t understand.
