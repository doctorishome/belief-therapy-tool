Spiritual/Religious False Belief #118 · The End Times

“I could be left behind because I’m not good enough.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Movies and books about being left behind. Childhood panic when family members could not be found. Teaching that your behavior determines whether you will be taken. Shame about sin. Anxiety about salvation. Christians hold different views about the order of end-times events, but the deeper fear here is about being rejected by God.

What Scripture teaches

Whatever view of the end times you hold, Scripture is clear that believers are kept by God, not by being good enough. God has not appointed us to suffer wrath but to obtain salvation through Jesus. No one can snatch Christ’s sheep out of His hand. There is no condemnation for those in Christ Jesus. John wrote so that believers may know they have eternal life. Your place with Christ rests on His goodness, not yours.

For God has not appointed us to suffer wrath, but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ. He died for us so that, whether we are awake or asleep, we may live together with Him (1 Thessalonians 5:9–10).

Also see

I give them eternal life, and they will never perish. No one can snatch them out of My hand. My Father who has given them to Me is greater than all. No one can snatch them out of My Father’s hand (John 10:28–29).

Therefore, there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus (Romans 8:1).

I have written these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God, so that you may know that you have eternal life (1 John 5:13).

For it is by grace you have been saved through faith, and this not from yourselves; it is the gift of God, not by works, so that no one can boast (Ephesians 2:8–9).

Common self-statements

  • When Jesus comes, I’ll be left behind.
  • I’m not holy enough to be taken.
  • I panic when I can’t find my family.
  • One sin could make me miss it.
  • I have to be good enough to be ready.
  • God will leave me because of my failures.

Common logical errors

  • Catastrophizing: Imagining being abandoned by God at His return.
  • Performance-based thinking: Believing your readiness depends on being good enough.
  • Emotional reasoning: Concluding “I feel unworthy, so I will be rejected.”
  • Discounting God’s promises: Overlooking promises that believers are kept by Christ.

Challenging questions

  • What has God appointed believers to, according to 1 Thessalonians 5:9?
  • Can anyone snatch me out of Jesus’ hand?
  • Is there condemnation for those in Christ?
  • Does my salvation rest on my goodness or Christ’s?
  • Why did John write 1 John 5:13?
  • Where did this fear first come from?
  • Have I trusted Jesus as my Savior?

Truth summary

I will not be left behind because I’m not good enough. My salvation rests on Jesus, not my goodness. God has not appointed me to wrath but to salvation through Christ. No one can snatch me out of His hand, and there is no condemnation for me in Him. I can know that I have eternal life.
