Spiritual/Religious False Belief #8 · Christ

“Jesus is one good way to God among many.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

A culture that values tolerance and says every path leads to the same place. Friends or family of other faiths whom you love and respect. Fear of seeming narrow or judgmental. Teachers or media that present Jesus only as a wise moral teacher. Discomfort with what Jesus’ claims mean for people you care about.

What Scripture teaches

Jesus did not present Himself as one teacher among many. He said He is the way, the truth, and the life, and that no one comes to the Father except through Him. The apostles taught that salvation is found in no other name. This is not arrogance on our part. It is God’s gracious provision: He sent His Son so that anyone, from any background, can come to Him through Christ.

Jesus answered, ‘I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me.’ (John 14:6).

Also see

Salvation exists in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given to men by which we must be saved (Acts 4:12).

For there is one God, and there is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus, who gave Himself as a ransom for all—the testimony that was given at just the right time (1 Timothy 2:5–6).

I am the gate. If anyone enters through Me, he will be saved. He will come in and go out and find pasture (John 10:9).

And this is that testimony: God has given us eternal life, and this life is in His Son. Whoever has the Son has life; whoever does not have the Son of God does not have life (1 John 5:11–12).

Common self-statements

  • All religions are basically the same.
  • It doesn’t matter what you believe as long as you’re sincere.
  • Saying Jesus is the only way is unloving.
  • Jesus was a great teacher, nothing more.
  • Good people will get to God however they choose.
  • I don’t want to seem narrow-minded.

Common logical errors

  • Emotional reasoning: Deciding what is true based on what feels kind or comfortable.
  • Overgeneralization: Assuming that because religions share some values, they all lead to the same God.
  • Mind reading: Assuming people will see you as hateful if you believe Jesus’ own words.
  • Selective attention: Accepting Jesus’ moral teaching while ignoring what He said about Himself.
  • False dilemma: Believing you must choose between loving people and believing Jesus is the only way.

Challenging questions

  • What did Jesus Himself say about the way to the Father?
  • Can Jesus be only a good teacher if He claimed to be the only way?
  • Is something true because it feels tolerant, or because it is true?
  • Can I believe Jesus is the only way and still love and respect people of other faiths?
  • Why would God send His Son to die if there were many other ways?
  • Have I let culture, rather than Scripture, shape this belief?
  • What does the evidence of Jesus’ resurrection suggest about His claims?

Truth summary

Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life. God has provided one sure way to Himself, and it is open to everyone through Christ. I can hold this truth with humility and love, respecting and caring for people of every background while pointing them to the Savior who welcomes all who come.

  • Truth journal: Each day, read one thing Jesus said about Himself in the Gospel of John and write it down.

My action step this week

Do one thing that tests the lie in real life.
