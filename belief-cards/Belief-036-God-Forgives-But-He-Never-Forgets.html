Spiritual/Religious False Belief #36 · Forgiveness

“God forgives, but He never forgets.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

People in your life who said they forgave you but kept bringing up the past. A parent who held old mistakes over your head. Your own memory, which replays past sins vividly. Thinking that because God knows everything, He must be holding your sins against you. Fear that past failures will be used against you later.

What Scripture teaches

God is all-knowing, but Scripture says He chooses not to hold forgiven sins against us. “I will remember their sins no more” means He will never bring them up against you again. He blots out transgressions for His own sake, removes them as far as the east is from the west, and casts them into the depths of the sea. When God forgives, the case is closed.

I, yes I, am He who blots out your transgressions for My own sake and remembers your sins no more (Isaiah 43:25).

Then He adds: ‘Their sins and lawless acts I will remember no more.’ And where these have been forgiven, an offering for sin is no longer needed (Hebrews 10:17–18).

As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us (Psalm 103:12).

Who is a God like You, who pardons iniquity and passes over the transgression of the remnant of His inheritance— who does not retain His anger forever, because He delights in loving devotion? He will again have compassion on us; He will vanquish our iniquities. You will cast out all our sins into the depths of the sea (Micah 7:18–19).

No longer will each man teach his neighbor or his brother, saying, ‘Know the LORD,’ because they will all know Me, from the least of them to the greatest, declares the LORD. For I will forgive their iniquities and will remember their sins no more (Jeremiah 31:34).

Common self-statements

  • God is keeping a record of everything I’ve done.
  • Someday He’ll bring it all up again.
  • I may be forgiven, but God still sees me as that person.
  • My past will always count against me.
  • God remembers, so I can never really be clean.
  • He forgave me, but He’s still disappointed.

Common logical errors

  • Projection: Picturing God like people who forgave you but kept score.
  • Fortune telling: Predicting that God will hold your past against you later.
  • Mental filter: Focusing on God’s knowledge while ignoring His promise not to remember.
  • Emotional reasoning: Concluding “I can’t forget it, so God must not either.”

Challenging questions

  • What does God say He will do with my forgiven sins?
  • Whose memory am I really afraid of: God’s or people’s?
  • If God casts my sins into the sea, will He go fishing for them?
  • Why does God say He blots out my sins “for My own sake”?
  • Is my own memory the same as God’s judgment?
  • What does “as far as the east is from the west” mean to me?
  • What would change if I believed my case is closed?

Truth summary

God does not hold my forgiven sins against me. He knows all things, but He promises to remember my sins no more. He has blotted them out, removed them as far as the east is from the west, and cast them into the depths of the sea. My forgiven past will never be used against me by God.
