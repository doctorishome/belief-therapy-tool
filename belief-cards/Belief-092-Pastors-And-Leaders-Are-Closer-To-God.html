Spiritual/Religious False Belief #92 · Church & Authority

“Pastors and leaders are closer to God than I am.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Seeing leaders on a stage and feeling distant from them. Church cultures that treat clergy as a higher class of Christian. Feeling unqualified to pray or serve. Assuming that spiritual titles mean spiritual closeness. Your own sense of unworthiness.

What Scripture teaches

All believers are a royal priesthood, a people belonging to God. Every Christian can approach the throne of grace with confidence. Jesus told His disciples that they have one Teacher and one Father, and that they are all brothers and sisters. Righteousness comes through faith in Jesus to all who believe, with no distinction. Leaders have important roles, but they are not closer to God by position. Your access to God is as full as theirs.

But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God’s own possession, to proclaim the virtues of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvelous light (1 Peter 2:9).

Also see

Let us then approach the throne of grace with confidence, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help us in our time of need (Hebrews 4:16).

But you are not to be called ‘Rabbi,’ for you have one Teacher, and you are all brothers. And do not call anyone on earth your father, for you have one Father, who is in heaven. Nor are you to be called instructors, for you have one Instructor, the Christ (Matthew 23:8–10).

There is neither Jew nor Greek, slave nor free, male nor female, for you are all one in Christ Jesus (Galatians 3:28).

And this righteousness from God comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe. There is no distinction, for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God (Romans 3:22–23).

Common self-statements

  • Pastors have a special connection to God that I don’t.
  • God listens to leaders more than me.
  • I’m just an ordinary church member.
  • My prayers don’t count as much as theirs.
  • I need a leader to hear from God for me.
  • I’ll never be as close to God as they are.

Common logical errors

  • Comparison: Measuring your closeness to God by someone else’s role.
  • Confusing role with relationship: Assuming a title means greater access to God.
  • Discounting yourself: Treating your relationship with God as second-class.
  • Labeling: Calling yourself “just ordinary.”

Challenging questions

  • What does 1 Peter 2:9 call all believers?
  • Who can approach God’s throne with confidence?
  • What did Jesus say about titles in Matthew 23?
  • Is there any distinction in how people are made right with God?
  • Does a leader’s role give them more access to God?
  • What does my own relationship with God look like?
  • How can I draw near to God today?

Truth summary

Pastors and leaders are not closer to God than I am because of their role. I am part of a royal priesthood. Through Jesus, I can approach God’s throne with confidence. Leaders serve important roles, but we are all brothers and sisters with one Father and one Teacher. My access to God is full and free.
