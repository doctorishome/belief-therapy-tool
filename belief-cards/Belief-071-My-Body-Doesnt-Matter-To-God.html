Spiritual/Religious False Belief #71 · Resurrection & the Body

“My body doesn’t matter to God; only my soul counts.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Teaching that focused only on “saving souls.” Ideas that the body is a prison and the spirit is what really matters. Neglecting health, rest, or care because it seemed unspiritual. Shame about the body. Seeing physical needs as less important than spiritual ones.

What Scripture teaches

God cares about the whole person: spirit, soul, and body. Paul prayed that believers would be kept blameless in spirit, soul, and body. Your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit, and you are called to offer it to God as a living sacrifice. Jesus rose bodily from the dead, and God promises to give life to your mortal body and transform it to be like Christ’s glorious body. Your body matters to God now and forever.

Now may the God of peace Himself sanctify you completely, and may your entire spirit, soul, and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ (1 Thessalonians 5:23).

Also see

Therefore I urge you, brothers, on account of God’s mercy, to offer your bodies as living sacrifices, holy and pleasing to God, which is your spiritual service of worship (Romans 12:1).

Do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit who is in you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own; you were bought at a price. Therefore glorify God with your body (1 Corinthians 6:19–20).

And if the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus from the dead is living in you, He who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through His Spirit, who lives in you (Romans 8:11).

But our citizenship is in heaven, and we eagerly await a Savior from there, the Lord Jesus Christ, who, by the power that enables Him to subject all things to Himself, will transform our lowly bodies to be like His glorious body (Philippians 3:20–21).

Common self-statements

  • Only my soul matters to God.
  • Taking care of my body is unspiritual.
  • My body is just a shell.
  • It doesn’t matter how I treat my body.
  • God only cares about spiritual things.
  • My physical needs aren’t important.

Common logical errors

  • All-or-nothing thinking: Separating body and soul as if only one matters.
  • Discounting: Treating physical care as unimportant to faith.
  • Mental filter: Overlooking God’s plan to raise and transform the body.
  • Neglect: Ignoring rest, health, or care in the name of spirituality.

Challenging questions

  • Why did Paul pray for spirit, soul, and body?
  • What does it mean that my body is a temple of the Holy Spirit?
  • Did Jesus rise with a body or only as a spirit?
  • What does God promise to do with my mortal body?
  • How might I honor God with my body this week?
  • Who taught me that the body doesn’t matter?
  • What physical need have I been neglecting?

Truth summary

My body matters to God. He cares about my whole person: spirit, soul, and body. My body is a temple of the Holy Spirit, and I can offer it to God as a living sacrifice. One day God will raise and transform my body to be like Christ’s glorious body. I can care for my body as an act of worship.
