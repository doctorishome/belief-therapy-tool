Spiritual/Religious False Belief #70 · Hell

“Fear of hell is the main reason to follow God.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Coming to faith mainly through frightening sermons about hell. Teaching that used fear to motivate obedience. Following God out of fear without ever experiencing His love. Seeing God mainly as a judge to avoid. Worry that without fear, you would stop obeying.

What Scripture teaches

Scripture is honest about judgment, and a healthy reverence for God is good. But the main reason to follow God is His love. There is no fear in love; perfect love drives out fear. We love because He first loved us. It is God’s kindness that leads us to repentance. Christ’s love compels us. You did not receive a spirit of slavery that leads to fear, but the Spirit of sonship, by whom we cry, “Abba! Father!”

There is no fear in love, but perfect love drives out fear, because fear involves punishment. The one who fears has not been perfected in love. We love because He first loved us (1 John 4:18–19).

As the Father has loved Me, so have I loved you. Remain in My love. If you keep My commandments, you will remain in My love, just as I have kept My Father’s commandments and remain in His love. I have told you these things so that My joy may be in you and your joy may be complete (John 15:9–11).

Common self-statements

  • I follow God so I don’t go to hell.
  • If I weren’t afraid, I’d stop obeying.
  • God is mainly someone to avoid angering.
  • Love is nice, but fear is what keeps me in line.
  • I don’t really enjoy God; I just fear Him.
  • Fear is the only thing that works for me.

Common logical errors

  • Mental filter: Focusing on judgment while overlooking God’s love.
  • Fear-based thinking: Believing fear is the strongest motivator for obedience.
  • Projection: Seeing God as a harsh authority figure.
  • All-or-nothing thinking: Thinking that without fear, you would have no reason to follow God.

Challenging questions

  • What does perfect love do with fear, according to 1 John 4:18?
  • Why do we love God, according to 1 John 4:19?
  • What leads us to repentance, according to Romans 2:4?
  • What kind of spirit did I receive: slavery to fear or sonship?
  • Is my obedience coming from love or from fear?
  • What would following God out of love look like for me?
  • Have I experienced God’s love, or only heard about His judgment?

Truth summary

Fear of hell is not the main reason to follow God. I follow Him because He first loved me. His kindness leads me to repentance, and His love compels me. I am not a slave living in fear but a child crying, “Abba! Father!” Perfect love drives out fear.
