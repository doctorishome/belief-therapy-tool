Sociocultural False Belief #41 · Mental Health and Seeking Help

“Strong people don't show emotion.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Families, sports, the military, workplaces, and some churches praise those who “keep it together.” Boys especially hear “Don’t cry.” We learn to bottle feelings, which then leak out as anger, numbness, or physical tension.

What Scripture teaches

“Jesus wept.” Isaiah calls Him “a man of sorrows, acquainted with grief.” Paul says, “Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep.” David wrote, “I am weary from groaning; all night I flood my bed with weeping.” Paul also says, “when I am weak, then I am strong.” The Lord who is strongest shows emotion. Strength is not the absence of feeling. It is facing feeling honestly and bringing it to God and to safe people.

Jesus wept (John 11:35).

Also see

He was despised and rejected by men, a man of sorrows, acquainted with grief. Like one from whom men hide their faces, He was despised, and we esteemed Him not (Isaiah 53:3).

Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep (Romans 12:15).

I am weary from groaning; all night I flood my bed with weeping and drench my couch with tears (Psalm 6:6).

But He said to me, ‘My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is perfected in weakness.’ Therefore I will boast all the more gladly in my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may rest on me. That is why, for the sake of Christ, I delight in weaknesses, in insults, in hardships, in persecutions, in difficulties. For when I am weak, then I am strong (2 Corinthians 12:9–10).

Common self-statements

  • Tears are weakness.
  • I have to hold it together.
  • Feelings get in the way.
  • I should not need anyone.
  • Showing emotion makes me a burden.
  • I will fall apart if I let it out.

Common logical errors

  • All-or-nothing thinking: Believing I am either composed or weak.
  • Mind reading: Assuming people would think less of me.
  • Emotional reasoning: Feeling vulnerable and concluding that it is dangerous.
  • Fortune telling: Predicting I will lose control.

Challenging questions

  • Who taught me that feelings are weakness?
  • How did Jesus show emotion?
  • What happens to the feelings I hold in?
  • Who is safe enough to share with?
  • What would a friend say to someone who felt this way?
  • What is one feeling I could name this week?

Truth summary

Emotions are part of how God made me. Jesus wept, and strength includes honesty. I can name my feelings, bring them to God, and share them with safe people.

Pick one or two that fit me
  • Named feelings: Each day, name one feeling and tell God about it.
