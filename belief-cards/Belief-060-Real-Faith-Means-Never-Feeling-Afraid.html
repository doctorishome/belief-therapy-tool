Spiritual/Religious False Belief #60 · Faith & Belief

“Real faith means never feeling afraid.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Hearing “Fear not” verses used to shame you for feeling afraid. Anxiety that feels like spiritual failure. Being told that fear is the opposite of faith. Comparing yourself with people who seem fearless. Not knowing that fear is a normal human response that God addresses with comfort.

What Scripture teaches

God often says “Do not fear” because He knows we feel afraid. These words are comfort, not condemnation. David said, “I will fear no evil, for You are with me,” in the valley of the shadow of death. When Peter was afraid and began to sink, Jesus immediately reached out and caught him. Faith is not the absence of fear; it is turning to God when we are afraid.

Do not fear, for I am with you; do not be afraid, for I am your God. I will strengthen you; I will surely help you; I will uphold you with My right hand of righteousness (Isaiah 41:10).

Also see

I sought the LORD, and He answered me; He delivered me from all my fears (Psalm 34:4).

But when he saw the strength of the wind, he was afraid, and beginning to sink, cried out, ‘Lord, save me!’ Immediately Jesus reached out His hand and took hold of Peter. ‘You of little faith,’ He said, ‘why did you doubt?’ (Matthew 14:30–31).

Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil, for You are with me; Your rod and Your staff, they comfort me (Psalm 23:4).

Peace I leave with you; My peace I give to you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled; do not be afraid (John 14:27).

Common self-statements

  • If I had real faith, I wouldn’t be afraid.
  • My anxiety is a sin.
  • God is disappointed when I’m scared.
  • I have to hide my fear from other Christians.
  • Being afraid means I don’t trust God.
  • Strong Christians are never anxious.

Common logical errors

  • All-or-nothing thinking: Believing you are either fearless or faithless.
  • Should statements: “I should never feel afraid.”
  • Misreading Scripture: Hearing “Do not fear” as a scolding instead of a comfort.
  • Labeling: Calling yourself faithless because you feel anxious.

Challenging questions

  • Why does God so often say “Do not fear”?
  • What did Jesus do when Peter was afraid and sinking?
  • Did David feel no fear, or did he trust God in it?
  • Is fear a sin, or a feeling I can bring to God?
  • What does Psalm 34:4 say happened when David sought the LORD?
  • Could anxiety have physical or emotional causes I can address?
  • What is one fear I can bring to God right now?

Truth summary

Real faith does not mean never feeling afraid. God says “Do not fear” to comfort me, because He knows I am afraid. When I am afraid, I can turn to Him. He is with me, He strengthens me, and He holds me with His right hand. Faith is trusting God in my fear.
