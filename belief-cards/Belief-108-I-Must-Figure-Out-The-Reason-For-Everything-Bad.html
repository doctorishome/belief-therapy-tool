Spiritual/Religious False Belief #108 · Suffering

“I must figure out the reason for everything bad that happens.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

A need for control and certainty. Anxiety that searches for answers to feel safe. Teaching that every event has a clear, knowable purpose. Painful events that seem senseless. Feeling that you cannot move on until you understand.

What Scripture teaches

God has purposes in all things, but He does not always reveal them to us. The secret things belong to the LORD, while the things revealed belong to us. Even the wise cannot comprehend all of God’s work under the sun. Jesus told Peter, “You do not realize now what I am doing, but later you will understand.” Like a weaned child resting with its mother, we can quiet our souls before God without needing every answer.

The secret things belong to the LORD our God, but the things revealed belong to us and to our children forever, so that we may follow all the words of this law (Deuteronomy 29:29).

Also see

My heart is not proud, O LORD, my eyes are not haughty. I do not aspire to great things or matters too lofty for me. Surely I have stilled and quieted my soul; like a weaned child with his mother, like a weaned child is my soul within me (Psalm 131:1–2).

I saw every work of God, and that a man is unable to comprehend the work that is done under the sun. Despite his efforts to search it out, he cannot find its meaning; even if the wise man claims to know, he is unable to comprehend (Ecclesiastes 8:17).

Jesus replied, ‘You do not realize now what I am doing, but later you will understand.’ (John 13:7).

‘For My thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways My ways,’ declares the LORD. ‘For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so My ways are higher than your ways and My thoughts than your thoughts.’ (Isaiah 55:8–9).

Common self-statements

  • I can’t rest until I know why this happened.
  • There must be a reason, and I have to find it.
  • If I understood, I could accept it.
  • Not knowing means God doesn’t care.
  • I keep replaying it looking for answers.
  • Other people seem to understand their suffering.

Common logical errors

  • Intolerance of uncertainty: Needing to understand everything before you can have peace.
  • Rumination: Going over events again and again looking for answers.
  • Mind reading: Assuming you can know God’s specific reasons.
  • Control-seeking: Trying to manage pain by figuring it out.

Challenging questions

  • What does Deuteronomy 29:29 say about secret things?
  • Can even the wise understand all of God’s work?
  • What did Jesus tell Peter about understanding later?
  • Has searching for reasons brought me peace or more anxiety?
  • Can I trust God without knowing why?
  • What does it mean to quiet my soul like a weaned child?
  • What has God revealed that I can act on today?

Truth summary

I do not have to figure out the reason for everything bad that happens. Some things belong to God alone. I may understand later, or not in this life. I can quiet my soul before God, trust His character, and focus on what He has revealed and what I can do today.

My practice plan

  • Make my Truth Card (below). Carry it and read it at least three times a day.
  • Memorize my Scripture from Step 4.
  • Listen to one song each day from “Songs that speak truth to this belief” on my card, and fill in my song journal (below).
  • Set reminders to silently repeat my truth. Check one or more: ☐ A buzz: my watch or phone vibrates every ______ minutes
    ☐ An everyday event: every time I ______________________________
    ☐ A visual reminder: something I will see often, such as a sticky note on my mirror, my truth as the background picture on my phone, or a note on my car dashboard.
Pick one or two that fit me
  • Let-it-rest log: When I start searching for “why,” write the question, then write: “This belongs to God,” and one thing I can do today.
  • My name in Scripture: Rewrite my verse with my name in it and read it aloud each morning. For example, Psalm 131:2 becomes: “Surely Sarah has stilled and quieted her soul; like a weaned child with her mother, like a weaned child is her soul within her.”
  • Gratitude journal: Each night, write three things I thank God for.
  • Truth recording: Record my own voice reading my Truth Card and verse, and listen in the car or on a walk.
  • Reading: The “Trust” topic in Pearls.
  • Listening: The Overcoming Anxiety audio by Dr. Brian Campbell. Tap to play.
6

My action step this week

Do one thing that tests the lie in real life.

  • Set a 15-minute “worry time” each day for questions about why; outside that time, gently set them aside.
  • Read Psalm 131 slowly each morning, and practice resting quietly for two minutes.
  • Write a letter to God with your questions, and end with “I trust You.”
  • Talk with a counselor if searching for answers is keeping you stuck.
