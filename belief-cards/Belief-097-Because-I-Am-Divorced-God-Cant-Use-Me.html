Spiritual/Religious False Belief #97 · Marriage, Sex & Family

“Because I am divorced, God can’t use me.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Church policies that kept divorced people from serving. Shame or judgment from other Christians. Grief over a marriage that ended, whether or not it was your choice. Verses about divorce used without grace. Feeling like a failure or second-class Christian.

What Scripture teaches

God takes marriage seriously, and divorce is painful. But divorce is not beyond God’s grace or His purposes. God says, “Behold, I am about to do something new.” Jesus spoke with a Samaritan woman who had been married five times, and many in her town believed because of her testimony. God promises to repay the years the locusts have eaten, and to work all things together for good for those who love Him. Your story is not over.

‘Do not call to mind the former things; pay no attention to the things of old. Behold, I am about to do something new; even now it is coming. Do you not see it? Indeed, I will make a way in the wilderness and streams in the desert.’ (Isaiah 43:18–19).

Also see

Many of the Samaritans from that town believed in Jesus because of the woman’s testimony, ‘He told me everything I ever did.’ (John 4:39).

I will repay you for the years eaten by locusts— the swarming locust, the young locust, the destroying locust, and the devouring locust— My great army that I sent against you (Joel 2:25).

And we know that God works all things together for the good of those who love Him, who are called according to His purpose (Romans 8:28).

Therefore if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away. Behold, the new has come! (2 Corinthians 5:17).

Common self-statements

  • I’m damaged goods.
  • God can’t use someone who failed at marriage.
  • I’m a second-class Christian now.
  • My divorce disqualifies me forever.
  • I have nothing to offer God.
  • Everyone at church sees me as a failure.

Common logical errors

  • Labeling: Defining yourself by one painful event.
  • Overgeneralization: Turning “my marriage ended” into “my usefulness to God ended.”
  • Mind reading: Assuming everyone sees you as a failure.
  • Discounting God’s grace: Overlooking God’s pattern of using broken people.

Challenging questions

  • Whom did Jesus use to reach a whole town in John 4?
  • What does God say He is about to do in Isaiah 43:19?
  • Does God work only through people with perfect histories?
  • What has God taught me through this pain that could help others?
  • Have I brought any part of this I need to confess to God?
  • Is the judgment I fear coming from God or from people?
  • How might God want to use me now?

Truth summary

My divorce does not disqualify me from being used by God. He is doing something new in my life. God used a woman with a broken history to bring a whole town to Jesus, and He can use me. He can restore what was lost and work even this together for good.
