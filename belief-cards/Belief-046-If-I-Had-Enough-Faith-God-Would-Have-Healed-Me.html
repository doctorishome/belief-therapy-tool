Spiritual/Religious False Belief #46 · Miracles & Blessing

“If I had enough faith, God would have healed me.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Teaching that God always heals when faith is strong enough. Being told your illness continues because you doubt. Seeing others healed and wondering what is wrong with you. Praying hard for healing that has not come. Well-meaning people who said, “Just believe and you’ll be well.”

What Scripture teaches

God can heal, and we are free to ask Him. But Scripture does not teach that every illness lasts because of weak faith. Paul, a man of great faith, pleaded three times for his thorn to be removed, and God answered, “My grace is sufficient for you.” Paul left his co-worker Trophimus sick in Miletus. God works all things together for good for those who love Him, sometimes by healing and sometimes by giving strength in weakness.

Three times I pleaded with the Lord to take it away from me. But He said to me, ‘My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is perfected in weakness.’ Therefore I will boast all the more gladly in my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ may rest on me (2 Corinthians 12:8–9).

Also see

Erastus has remained at Corinth, and Trophimus I left sick in Miletus (2 Timothy 4:20).

And we know that God works all things together for the good of those who love Him, who are called according to His purpose (Romans 8:28).

My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion forever (Psalm 73:26).

I know how to live humbly, and I know how to abound. I am accustomed to any and every situation—to being filled and being hungry, to having plenty and having need. I can do all things through Christ who gives me strength (Philippians 4:12–13).

Common self-statements

  • My illness is my fault for not believing enough.
  • God would heal me if my faith were stronger.
  • Other people have more faith than I do.
  • I must be doing something wrong spiritually.
  • If I admit I’m sick, I’m confessing doubt.
  • God is disappointed in my weak faith.

Common logical errors

  • Personalization: Blaming your faith for an illness with many causes.
  • Overgeneralization: Turning “God sometimes heals in answer to faith” into “God always heals when faith is enough.”
  • Comparison: Measuring your faith by other people’s healing stories.
  • Mental filter: Ignoring faithful people in Scripture who were not healed.

Challenging questions

  • Did Paul lack faith when his thorn was not removed?
  • Why did Paul leave Trophimus sick?
  • What did God say to Paul instead of healing him?
  • Is my illness a measure of my faith, or part of living in a broken world?
  • Can God be glorified through my weakness?
  • Who taught me that faith always brings healing?
  • What does it look like to trust God while I am still sick?

Truth summary

My illness is not proof that my faith is too weak. God can heal, and I can ask Him to. But even faithful people like Paul were not always healed. God’s grace is sufficient for me, and His power is made perfect in my weakness. I can trust Him whether He heals me now, later, or in eternity.
