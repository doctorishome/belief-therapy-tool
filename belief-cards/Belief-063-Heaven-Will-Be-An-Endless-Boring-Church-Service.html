Spiritual/Religious False Belief #63 · Heaven

“Heaven will be an endless, boring church service.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Where it often comes from

Pictures of heaven as clouds, harps, and endless singing. Long, dull church services in childhood. Never hearing much about what heaven is really like. Thinking of eternity as time dragging on forever. Loving life on earth and fearing heaven will be less.

What Scripture teaches

Scripture describes heaven as far richer than we can imagine: no eye has seen what God has prepared for those who love Him. In God’s presence is fullness of joy and eternal pleasures. God promises a new heaven and a new earth, a holy city, a great banquet, and His people reigning with Him. Worship there will not be dull duty but the joy of seeing God’s face. Everything good on earth is only a shadow of what is coming.

Rather, as it is written: ‘No eye has seen, no ear has heard, no heart has imagined, what God has prepared for those who love Him.’ (1 Corinthians 2:9).

There will be no more night in the city, and they will have no need for the light of a lamp or of the sun. For the Lord God will shine on them, and they will reign forever and ever (Revelation 22:3–5).

Common self-statements

  • Heaven sounds boring.
  • I’d rather stay on earth.
  • Forever singing hymns? No thanks.
  • I’ll miss all the good things here.
  • Eternity will feel like it never ends, in a bad way.
  • I feel guilty that heaven doesn’t excite me.

Common logical errors

  • Limited imagination: Picturing heaven only through dull childhood images.
  • Projection: Assuming heaven will feel like the most boring parts of church.
  • Mental filter: Overlooking Scripture’s pictures of feasting, beauty, and joy.
  • Fortune telling: Predicting you will be bored without knowing what God has prepared.

Challenging questions

  • What does 1 Corinthians 2:9 say about what God has prepared?
  • What does Psalm 16:11 say is in God’s presence?
  • What images does Scripture give of heaven: a banquet, a city, a new earth?
  • Would seeing God’s face be boring?
  • Is my picture of heaven from the Bible or from cartoons?
  • What is the best moment of my life, and could heaven be better?
  • What am I looking forward to most about being with God?

Truth summary

Heaven will not be boring. No eye has seen what God has prepared for those who love Him. In His presence is fullness of joy and pleasures forever. God promises a new heaven and earth, a great feast, and the joy of seeing His face. The best things here are only a shadow of what is coming.
